# 2. Why this milestone

The roadmap's Phase 4 still owes the Phase 5 screen designs and the navigation decision (0.10 deferred them "to the start of Phase 5 (0.11)"). Of Phase 5's features, only **History for any past day** needs no undecided product policy, no backend change and no migration: `GET /v1/days/{date}` already serves any date, but the app only ever asks for today. Settings, goals and macros wait for the calorie-goal bounds decision; meal edit waits for the editing decision and needs `PATCH` and a migration.

# 8. Physical acceptance (proposed)

On a scratchpad copy of the real DB, served by `python app.py` from the worktree; hashes of the real DB and backups checked before and after; light and dark.

1. Launch: Today unchanged; History opens on today, matching Today.
2. Step back to 2026-09-25: its meals, totals and goal (2633).
3. Date picker → 2026-09-01, then a date with no meals: correct meals, then an empty state naming the date.
4. Next and the picker offer no future date.
5. Delete a past meal: row gone, that day's totals update, Today unchanged.
6. Log a meal on Today while History is on a past day: the past day unchanged; History's Today shows it.
7. Delete today's meal in History: Today's totals update.
8. Server stopped, switch days, pull to refresh: error with Retry; no day shows another day's meals; Retry recovers.
9. Background and return: the selected date kept; refresh works.
10. Largest accessibility text size: header, controls and rows readable, nothing clipped.
11. VoiceOver: date, previous/next, Today, date picker and rows announce meaningfully; delete via the Actions rotor.
12. Regression: text and photo logging, Root error screen, Reduce Motion ring.
